Solers AI UI Icons

The functional icons under assets/ui_icons are vendored from Tabler Icons
v3.46.0 (commit 8ac7d81). Files are renamed to stable Solers semantic ids;
their upstream source names are listed below. Provider logos and project
resource previews are separate identity/content assets and are not Tabler UI
icons.
